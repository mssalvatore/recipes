# Waffles

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| All-purpose flour | 2 cups |
| Granulated sugar | 4 Tbsp |
| Baking powder | 4 tsp |
| Salt | $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp |
| Eggs | 2 eggs |
| Milk | 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups |
| Butter, melted | 6 Tbsp |
| Vanilla | 1 tsp |

## Directions

1. Preheat the waffle iron.

2. Whisk the eggs into the milk.

3. Combine the dry ingredients, then make a well in the middle.

4. Add in wet ingredients and stir just until mixed (don't overmix; it's 
   supposed to be lumpy!). 

5. Scoop batter (~$\frac{1}{2}$ cup at a time) into the waffle iron.

# Chicken Meatballs

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Ground chicken | 1 lb |
| Egg | 1 egg |
| Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| Breadcrumbs | $\frac{1}{2}$ cup |
| Grated parmesan | $\frac{1}{2}$ cup |
| Garlic powder | $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp |
| Onion powder | $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp |
| Salt | $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp |
| Pepper | to taste |

## Yield

~ 15 meatballs (double the recipe for larger families or for leftovers)

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients together, then roll into balls.

2. Heat oil in a frying pan and fry meatballs until evenly 
   browned and an internal temperature of 165 degrees is reached.

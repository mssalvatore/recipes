# Vegetable Corn Chowder

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Olive oil | ~1 Tbsp |
| Red onion, diced | 1 red onion |
| Red bell pepppr, diced | 1 pepper |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Potato | 1 large potato |
| All-purpose flour | 2 Tbsp |
| Milk | 2 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups |
| Chicken or vegetable stock | 1 $\frac{1}{4}$ cups |
| Broccoli | 1 $\frac{3}{4}$ oz / 50g broccoli florets |
| Canned corn, drained | 3 cups |
| Cheddar cheese, grated | $\frac{3}{4}$ cup (or to taste) |
| Salt and pepper | to taste |


## Directions

1. Heat the oil in a pan. Add the onion, pepper, garlic, and potato; cook over
   low heat, stirring often for 2–3 minutes.

2. Stir in the flour for 30 seconds. Gradually add in the milk and stock.

3. Add the broccoli and corn. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring constantly,
   then reduce the heat and simmer for about 20 minutes, until veggies are
   tender.

4. Stir in half of the cheese, until it melts.

5. Season to taste with salt and pepper, garnishing with the remaining cheese.
